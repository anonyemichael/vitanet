Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VitaNet (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the VitaNet mobile application and related websites (the “Service”). VitaNet is an AI health triage assistant that helps you consider self-care, pharmacist, or urgent-care next steps. It does not provide medical diagnoses.

1. Information we process

On-device health content

Symptom descriptions, chat messages, and triage history you create in the app are stored locally on your device so you can revisit past guidance. We design the Service so this personal health information is not uploaded to a remote health data warehouse.

Account information (if you sign in)

If you choose optional sign-in (for example Google Sign-In), we may process basic account identifiers such as name, email address, and profile photo provided by the authentication provider, solely to operate your account.

Website & enquiries

If you contact us through the Enquiries form or email, we receive the details you submit (such as name, email, and message) so we can respond.

Technical data

Like most apps and sites, limited technical logs (device type, app version, crash diagnostics) may be processed to keep the Service reliable and secure. These logs are not used to build a remote medical profile about you.

2. How we use information

  • To provide triage chat and on-device history features
  • To authenticate optional accounts
  • To respond to enquiries and support requests
  • To improve stability, security, and performance
  • To meet legal obligations where applicable

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal health information
  • We do not use your symptom chats for third-party advertising
  • We do not claim VitaNet replaces a licensed clinician

4. Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate authentication, hosting, or communications — under contracts that restrict use to providing those services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the public.

5. Retention

On-device history remains until you clear it or uninstall the app. Enquiry emails and account records are kept only as long as needed to respond and operate the Service, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure; please protect your device with a passcode or biometric lock.

7. Children’s privacy

VitaNet is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Clear local history from within the app or by uninstalling
  • Disconnect optional sign-in from your device settings / provider
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of enquiry or account data we hold

9. International users

If you access the Service from outside the country where our operators are located, your information may be processed in regions with different data-protection laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Service after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@vitanet.app or use our Enquiries page.

This page is provided for product transparency and is not legal advice. Have counsel review before public launch in your jurisdiction.
