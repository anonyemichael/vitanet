02 — Triage result

Three paths. Color-coded. Actionable.

Every check ends with Manage at Home, Visit a Pharmacist, or Seek Urgent Care — plus a summary, what to monitor, and a medical disclaimer.

  • Share your summary with someone who can help
  • Start a new check anytime
  • Urgent path surfaces emergency guidance
Manage at Home

Mild symptoms that appear stable for now.

Recommendation

Rest, hydrate, and monitor for 24–48 hours.

What to monitor

  • Fever rising above 38.5°C
  • Worsening headache or vomiting
  • New neurological symptoms
