FAQ

Answers before you download.

Is VitaNet a doctor or a diagnosis tool?

No. VitaNet provides triage recommendations only. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medicine, or replace professional medical advice.

What happens in a medical emergency?

If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your local emergency services immediately. Do not wait for an app response.

Where is my health data stored?

Symptom history is kept on your device. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Who is VitaNet for?

Adults who want a responsible first step when mild-to-moderate symptoms appear and are unsure whether to rest, visit a pharmacist, or seek urgent care.

How can I contact the team?

Use the Enquiries page for product questions, press, partnerships, or support.

Important

Medical disclaimer

VitaNet is designed to help you assess whether symptoms suggest self-care at home, a visit to a pharmacist, or urgent medical attention. It does not provide medical diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment plans. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. Never disregard professional medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something suggested by this application.
