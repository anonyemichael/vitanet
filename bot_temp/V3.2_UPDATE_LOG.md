## 1. Execution Bug Fixed
- **Issue:** Bot was finding setups but throwing an `AttributeError: module 'bridge_client' has no attribute 'order_send'`.
- **Fix:** Corrected the method call in `bot.py` from `bridge.order_send()` to the correct `bridge.place_order()`. Trades can now successfully execute.

## 2. Strategy Overhaul (v3.1 -> v3.2)
Historical analysis of 54 trades showed a 16.7% win rate, driven by lagging entries, tight stop losses on Gold, and a TP1 that was rarely hit. The following core strategy fixes were deployed to `strategy.py` and `config.py`:

- **Sniper Entries (15M CHoCH):** Removed the lagging `4H Engulfing` trigger (which entered *after* a move exhausted). Promoted the `15M CHoCH` to be the primary entry trigger when price enters the 4H AOI/OTE zone.
- **Volatile SL for Gold:** Gold (`XAUUSDm`) was previously treated like a low-volatility Forex pair, causing random wicks to hit the SL. It is now classified as volatile (alongside Crypto), receiving a deeper 12-candle lookback and a 0.5x ATR buffer.
- **TP1 Adjusted to 1:1:** Lowered `TP1_RR` from `1.2` to `1.0`. The bot now secures 50% partial profits exactly at a 1:1 risk-reward, massively increasing the partial win rate and allowing the SL to be moved to break-even earlier.
- **Risk Floor Lowered:** Lowered `MIN_RISK_USD` from `$3.50` to `$1.50` in `config.py`. A $3.50 minimum on a $27 balance was dangerously high (~13% risk). It is now capped at a safer ~5.5% minimum risk.
